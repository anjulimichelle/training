Ticket Assessment Hub

Paste ticket links to make a quiz form, see each trainee's answers, and score them with Claude against your own guidelines for each ticket.

About this tool and how it was built What it stores, how grading works, and what it never does

How to use it

  1. Quiz Generation: add a title and date, paste the ticket links and the trainee emails, then click Generate quiz form. Each link gets the same four questions, and the instructions go at the top.
  2. Already ran a quiz in Google Forms? Use Import an existing quiz in tab 1 and pick its response sheet. The page reads the tickets and answers so you can score them here; add the ticket links if you have them.
  3. Responses: pick the quiz and click Sync responses to read the form's response sheet. Each trainee's answers show by ticket. Sync again any time to pull in late submissions.
  4. Scoring: set points and write guidelines for each ticket, then score. Change a guideline any time and re-score to update scores, errors and feedback.

How the Google Form is made

The page writes a short Google Apps Script for the quiz. You paste it at script.google.com and run it in your own Google account. It makes the form closed and shared with no one, plus a response sheet. You open the form and send it yourself.

The page never creates, opens or sends a form on its own.

How scoring works

  • Claude scores one ticket at a time for all trainees together, using only the points and guidelines you wrote for that ticket.
  • It judges whether the actions and resolution are right, not the exact wording, and lists each error.
  • When you change a ticket's points or guidelines, its scores show as out of date until you re-score.
  • Your manual adjustments stay through re-scoring, with Claude's own score kept beside them.
  • Scoring runs on the Claude account of whoever clicks the button, which asks permission the first time.

Who can see what

Quizzes, answers, guidelines and scores are saved on this page. People it's shared with as Contributor or Editor can use it. Viewers and Commenters see nothing.

Responses are read from the quiz's response sheet through your own Google Sheets connector. When you write results, the page adds or replaces one tab named Scoring Results in that sheet. It never changes the Form Responses tab.

How it was built

Built with Claude as a single page published as a claude.ai artifact, in the same style as the Training Agenda Library. The source is in ticket-assessment-hub/artifact in the training GitHub repository.

Terms used on this page

C and CP
The customer and the cleaning professional.
CRM actions
The steps a specialist takes in the internal tools, such as notes, refunds and flags.
Points
The most a trainee can score on one ticket. The total is the sum across tickets; the score is the total as a percentage.
Scoring guidelines
The trainer's notes on what a correct answer includes and how the points split. Claude scores against only these.
Out of date (old)
A score made before the ticket's points or guidelines last changed. Re-score to update it.
Adjusted
A score a trainer changed by hand. It stays through re-scoring.
